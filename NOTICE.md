# Source attribution

InTrance is existing software by Bernd Dachwald and Andreas Ohndorf. Its original
notices and restrictions remain in `workflow/InTrance_modified/`. This private
research repository does not grant public redistribution rights or apply a new
blanket licence to that source.

The local extension uses established ideal-sail dynamics, direct multiple
shooting, CasADi and Ipopt. Numerical-method references are linked in
[CODE_MAP.md](CODE_MAP.md). Third-party papers and external ephemeris binaries
are supplied separately.
